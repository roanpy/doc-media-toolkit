# Code of Conduct / 行为准则

## Our standard / 我们的标准

Be respectful, constructive, and specific. Welcome people regardless of experience, identity, background, language, or ability. Critique ideas and code, not people. Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, deliberate exposure of private information, and sustained disruption are not accepted.

请保持尊重、建设性和具体。欢迎不同经验、身份、背景、语言和能力的参与者；讨论观点和代码，不攻击个人。骚扰、威胁、歧视性言论、故意披露他人隐私和持续扰乱协作均不可接受。

## Scope and enforcement / 适用范围与执行

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other project spaces. Maintainers may edit or remove content, reject contributions, temporarily limit participation, or permanently ban participants when behavior is harmful or unsafe.

本准则适用于仓库 Issue、Pull Request、讨论、审查及其他项目空间。对有害或不安全的行为，维护者可编辑或删除内容、拒绝贡献、临时限制参与或永久封禁。

Report conduct concerns privately through the contact method listed on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Do not expose personal information in a public issue. Maintainers will handle reports confidentially and avoid conflicts of interest where practical.

行为问题请通过维护者 GitHub 主页公开列出的联系方式私下报告，不要在公开 Issue 中披露个人信息。维护者会保密处理，并在可行时回避利益冲突。
